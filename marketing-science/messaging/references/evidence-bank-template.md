# Evidence-Bank Template

Create one evidence bank per project. Store it with the project source, not in Marketing
OS. The bank must make a claim easy to verify, reuse, expire, or reject.

## Header

- Project:
- Owner:
- Last verified:
- Re-check trigger or expiry:
- Allowed surfaces:
- Ruled source documents:

## Verified claims

| Claim | Primary source | Date | Sample or method | Verification depth | Limits | Cleared surfaces | Re-check |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Use the exact claim the source supports. Record whether the team read the primary source
or relied on secondary coverage. Flag vendor sponsorship, small samples, old data, narrow
geography, and any qualifier that must travel with the claim.

## Product and company facts

| Fact | Ruled internal source | Owner | Last confirmed | Surfaces |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Prices, availability, integrations, customer counts, product behavior, and named
workflows belong here. A current owner confirms each fact before launch.

## Permissions

| Asset or statement | Permission owner | Written approval | Allowed use | Expiry or limits |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## Rejected or expired claims

| Claim | Status | Reason | Replacement, if any |
|---|---|---|---|

Keep rejected claims visible so they do not return through another draft. A softened
version of an unsupported claim remains unsupported.

## Operating rules

- Research before drafting.
- Proof travels with the claim.
- Re-verify competitive claims inside the launch window.
- Use the same wording across surfaces unless the source supports a narrower version.
- Route every public claim through `launches/claims-clearance`.
