## Goal

Establish organizational memory as essential AI infrastructure.

Operators should audit where judgment disappears from their workflows. Executives should recognize that preserving decisions, corrections, and context is an operating responsibility. Builders should create systems that retain and improve judgment over time.

The primary decision metric is unique report downloads. Qualified workshop applications and net-new subscriptions are secondary outcomes. Shares containing specific workflow audits, corrections, or retained decisions are diagnostic signals. Yash reports each outcome separately without inventing attribution across them.

## Story

AI can help a company produce more while the company remembers less.

Outputs multiply, yet the reasoning behind decisions disappears into chats, prompts, documents, and individual heads. Teams repeat mistakes because their systems retain answers without retaining the corrections that made those answers useful.

Every believes durable AI-native work requires preserving and improving judgment. Working Memory explains the problem. The workshop helps operators build the practice.

## Campaign Concept: The Memory Audit

Every campaign beat exposes one place where organizational judgment disappears and asks:

1. What decision was made?
2. What corrected or improved it?
3. What must the organization remember next time?

Report excerpts diagnose the problem. Practitioner examples supply proof. Editorial pieces widen the argument. The workshop gives operators a place to redesign their own systems.
