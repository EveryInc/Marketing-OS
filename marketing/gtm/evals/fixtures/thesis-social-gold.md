# Goal
Make “after automation” a public conversation through Thesis Statements show Thesis: 2027 as the physical moment taking shape around it.
Social has two jobs every day during this launch:
- **Provoke:** Give people an idea worth engaging with about AI and work.
- **Promote:** Show who is participating, why Thesis matters, and how to apply.
Dan and Every both Provoke and Promote. Dan leans further into Provoke. Every leans further into Promote. The full campaign strategy and daily beats live in <mention-page url="https://app.notion.com/p/3b6ca4f355ac818fa739d2dd3a82d65d">Thesis: 2027 — GTM Launch + 2 Week Sustain</mention-page>.
# The Social Idea
**After Automation, _______?**
This is the common format for Thesis Statements, public replies, speaker points of view, and the questions Thesis will work through in person. It gives the campaign one recognizable prompt without making every post look or sound the same.
The launch meetings and offsite gave us four useful rules:
- Thesis Statement headlines must be surprising, specific, and forward-looking. The more specifically we can frame it to a builder, operator, or executive the better.
- Social and email have different jobs. Email asks directly for the application; social makes the ideas viral.
- Avoid a “warm simmer.” Each beat adds a new argument, person, disagreement, program detail, nomination, or reason to act.
- The launch should feel like a coordinated swarm: Dan, Every, contributors, speakers, email, editorial, and the websites all launch together. Social beats the drum post-launch.
# Actions and Behavior
We want people to:
- Feel extreme FOMO, first and foremost.
- Complete “After Automation, ______ ” publicly and submit a Thesis Statement.
- Share a published statement with their own reaction.
- Agree, disagree, or put two competing theses against each other.
- Nominate someone to speak or attend, with a reason.
- Apply to attend Thesis.
- Subscribe to Every.
- Speakers and contributors publish from their own accounts and answer replies.
- People at Every join the argument in their own voices.
