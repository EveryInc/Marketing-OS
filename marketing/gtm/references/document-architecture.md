# GTM Document Architecture

## Master plan

### Launch facts

State the launch date, campaign window, primary action, and secondary action. Keep this block factual.

### Goal

Describe the condition the launch should create. Translate it into intended behavior for each relevant audience. Every's common audience groups are Builders, Operators, Seekers, and Executives; use only the segments that matter.

Include one short success definition. Use exact targets only when the user or an authoritative source supplies them.

### Story

Open with the tension. Connect the company's established point of view to what is launching now. Preserve canonical language.

### Campaign concept

Name the creative and behavioral mechanism. Explain why it will make people participate, share, apply, subscribe, or buy. A useful concept directs copy, design, social behavior, and sequencing.

### Launch backbone

Explain the causal sequence in five to eight steps. Show how the idea moves from the company to credible participants, the public, and the offer.

### Launch production

List the work required to ship: audience communication, personal outreach, editorial coordination, websites, lifecycle flows, and launch assets. Name owners and approval paths when known.

### Launch sequence

Organize prelaunch work by date. Separate locking the plan, locking the experience, staging and QA, launch, and sustain.

### Drumbeat

Use one row per calendar day. For each day, show the strategic lanes, the specific beat, and any dependency. Avoid filler posts and forced editorial connections.

### Copy and design surfaces

Finish with a checklist of assets, messages, pages, emails, and states that must be written, designed, built, or tested.

### Open questions and sources

Keep unresolved decisions visible. Link the current sources that support the plan.

## Social brief

The social brief is a compressed creative brief, usually under 650 words:

1. **Story:** The public argument and social idea.
2. **Actions and behavior:** What audiences, participants, and employees should do.
3. **Ideas to discuss:** Specific formats, hooks, and participation mechanics.

Leave channel matrices, response rules, owner tables, measurement dashboards, and full asset inventories in the master plan unless requested.
