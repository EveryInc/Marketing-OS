# Every Marketing OS

This folder is the complete skill. Keep its directory structure intact; paths described
as repository-relative resolve from this folder, regardless of the current working directory.

Read [the foundation](foundation/marketing-os/SKILL.md), then load only the workflow
needed for the task. The four workflow entries and their specialist references are
bundled here; they do not require separate installation.

Follow the user's brand and current approved sources. Every's brand modules are context
for Every work, not defaults for another company. Files marked `## To do` are scaffolding,
not authority. Historical cases do not establish current product facts or performance.

External services (Slack, Notion, Drive, Kit, GitHub) and optional reviewers require the
host's connected tools or user-supplied exports. Check availability before relying on them.
Continue work that does not depend on missing access; identify the specific gap. Never
claim a benchmark ran, an integration worked, or a change was published without evidence.

Preserve explicit user constraints, settled decisions, and existing authorization.
Draft approval alone does not authorize sending, scheduling, or publication.
