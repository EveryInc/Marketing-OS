# Compound Marketing Strategy

## Purpose

Marketing work produces deliverables but discards much of the reasoning, context,
correction, and evidence that made them good. Every new project starts too close to
zero.

## Positioning

Every project must do two jobs: produce effective marketing now and improve the system
that produces the next project.

Compound Marketing preserves the decisions that matter, keeps them under human control,
and adds only the learning that survives real use.

## Users

**Primary:** A marketing leader working with AI. They are hiring Compound Marketing to
turn scattered company knowledge into strategy and GTM without surrendering authorship,
judgment, or final taste.

**First reuse audience:** Another marketer on the same team. They are hiring it to
inherit the strategy and operating knowledge without requiring the original author to
reconstruct the system.

## Boundaries

- V1 covers strategy, recurring marketing programs, GTM planning, evaluation, and
  durable learning.
- Design, art direction, asset production, and channel automation are deferred.
- Multi-agent pods are optional infrastructure. Agent count and "Level 7" language are
  not evidence that the system works.
- Synthetic examples and structural tests may catch regressions, but cannot prove that
  the marketing improved.
- Project facts, temporary preferences, and isolated results do not become durable
  doctrine automatically.

_Resist a change when:_ It adds machinery without improving the core loop or making the
system's effect easier to prove.

## Key metrics

- **Human review and integration burden:** Minutes spent correcting, reconciling, and
  approving each accepted strategy or GTM artifact; recorded in the run record.
- **First-pass acceptance:** Percentage of artifacts accepted without substantial
  strategic rewriting.
- **Critical defect rate:** Unsupported facts, source conflicts, diluted protected
  language, or changed decisions reaching human review.
- **Inherited-decision reuse:** Previously captured decisions or rules reused and
  accepted without being reconstructed.
- **Independent rerun success:** A teammate completes the workflow from its governed
  inputs without the original author rebuilding the brief or system.

The first publishable proof requires a comparable second run with at least 20% less
correction or review time and no regression in acceptance or critical defects.

## Tracks

### Context to Strategy

Resolve company truth, customer evidence, prior decisions, protected language, and open
questions into a governing strategy.

_Why it serves the approach:_ The system cannot compound what it cannot remember or
distinguish by authority.

### Strategy to Market

Turn the approved strategy into a GTM plan for a launch or a program brief for ongoing
marketing. Preserve the audience, idea, proof, action, ownership, and decision gate.

_Why it serves the approach:_ Strategy becomes valuable when it governs coherent action
without being diluted during translation.

### Market to Memory

Compare drafts with human edits, evaluate outcomes against predeclared measures,
classify the learning, and update the narrowest durable source.

_Why it serves the approach:_ Each completed project leaves the next project with better
judgment rather than more undifferentiated context.

## Brand

**One-liner:** Compound Marketing turns every marketing project into better work now and
a better system for the next one.

**Key message:** Preserve the decisions that mattered, put them to work, and use the
result to improve the next run.
