# Product context

A small AI software company has several internal documents describing a new meeting
assistant. Product says it summarizes calls and proposes follow-up tasks. Sales calls it
an executive copilot. The CEO calls it a replacement for status meetings. None of these
descriptions has been approved as positioning.

## Known facts

- The product records only when a user explicitly starts it.
- The current beta has 24 active teams.
- The product can draft tasks but cannot assign them automatically.
- The launch date is proposed for Oct. 6, 2026, but the product owner has not approved it.

## Customer evidence

- Three team leads say their main problem is losing decisions after meetings.
- One founder says the summaries save her time.
- No customer has called it an executive copilot.

## Human direction

- Primary audience: team leads at companies with 20-200 employees.
- Desired behavior: join the beta waitlist.
- Protected phrase: "Meetings should leave a memory."
- Do not make a launch calendar yet.

## Request

Use Compound Marketing to produce the decision record and the smallest governing
strategy. Preserve uncertainty rather than inventing decisions.
