# Every verbal identity

Source: Every Brand Book, final working edit, Aug. 19, 2026, verbal identity section.
This reference translates the ruled verbal identity into operating guidance. Douglas's
authored tonalities and surface rules are protected language.

## Brand character

Every's archetype is the Creator-Magician, expressed through Hermes: messenger, guide,
trickster, and god of thresholds. Hermes moves between the technical and human, the
theoretical and applied, the future and present. He can be undignified, unpretentious,
and silly, but never vulgar.

Technology is Hermetic. It can help or threaten, clarify or confuse. Every guides people
toward its useful, human application. We live in the future and want customers to join us.

### Translator

- Make the frontier understandable through use, tests, and examples.
- Explain enough context for a smart non-specialist to follow.
- Preserve complexity where it changes the decision.
- Connect technical detail to human or business consequence.

### Guide

- Walk with willing people through transitions.
- Show the route, tools, tradeoffs, and next step.
- Build judgment and self-sufficiency.
- Never evangelize, scold, or exploit anxiety about falling behind.

### Trickster

- Use wit and play to make rigorous work feel alive.
- Cross disciplinary and organizational boundaries when the crossing reveals something.
- Prefer a raised eyebrow to a punchline.
- Keep the work serious even when the form is playful.

## Operating worldview

### The world is made, not given

What we make helps determine what comes next, so we are responsible for it.

### Ideas and action make each other better

We use, build, and test before we recommend.

### Choose what creates more possibility

Fear and criticism contain useful information. We favor hope, generosity, and ideas that
give people something to act on.

### Technology should expand human agency

AI extends human work. Judgment, taste, responsibility, and the decision to make
something new remain in the human realm.

### See for yourself

We are trailblazers. To report back from the future, we need to go live in it.

## Voice requirements

- Conversational and authoritative
- Accessible without dumbing down
- Personal experience connected to wider cultural or economic stakes
- Confident, nuanced claims
- Direct address when useful
- Authenticity and vulnerability when personal stakes make abstract forces vivid

## Tone modes

### Lucid

Clear, unadorned, precise. Use no jargon without earned context. This is the default for
newsletters, product copy, documentation, and AI & I.

### Kinetic

Forward-leaning, urgent, and compressed. Use short declaratives and active voice. Avoid
exclamation points and breathless urgency. Use for launches, announcements, Studio, and
software.

### Intimate

First-person, specific, and sometimes vulnerable. Make the writer's or GM's stake visible.
Use for essays, camps, onboarding, inside-Every stories, events, and selected newsletters.

### Wry

Dry, observational, and slightly amused. Aim for a raised eyebrow rather than a laugh.
Use for commentary, social, asides, media, and selected Studio work.

## We are / we are not

| We are | We are not | Operational meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Incisive | Encyclopedic | Cut to the insight. Do not inventory the whole field or become dull, superficial, or wordy. |
| Action-oriented | Frantic | Stay in motion without becoming wild, uncontrolled, or chaotic. |
| Generous | Indiscriminate | Share freely with careful thought, judgment, and distinction. |
| Inventive | Gimmicky | Make new instruments and ways of thinking, not decoration or trend-chasing. |
| Grounded | Abstract | Revolve around something real. Be specific and clear-eyed. |
| Vernacular | Jargony | Speak the living language without dumbing it down or becoming technical for its own sake. |
| Playful | Flippant | Have fun without becoming casual or dismissive about serious work. |
| Curious | Anxious | Explore to uncover the truth, not because uncertainty creates panic. |
| Honest | Contrarian | Volunteer the uncomfortable truth without loving or rejecting an idea for attention. |
| Polished | Precious | Make effort and craft visible, then ship rather than perfecting forever. |

## Surface registers

### Ideas / Media

Mode: Lucid + Wry. Governing verb: **translate**.

- Lead with the signal.
- Say what changed in sentence one and why it matters by sentence three.
- Use a personal point of view earned through practice.
- Take a position. Neutral summary is insufficient when judgment is the product.

### Software

Mode: Kinetic + Lucid. Governing verb: **produce**.

- Speak maker to maker.
- Compress the explanation and show the object.
- Name what the product does in the reader's next minute.
- Use demos, specific features, and social proof instead of aspiration.
- Write with confidence and no superlatives.

### Education

Mode: Intimate + Kinetic. Governing verb: **equip**.

- Use second person by default.
- Teach by doing.
- Create urgency through consequence, never anxiety.
- Show instructors' current edge cases, experiments, or failures.
- End each unit with an action.

### Enterprise

Mode: Lucid + Intimate. Governing verb: **guide**.

- Start with the work that needs to change.
- Show the people, workflow, and outcome.
- Work beside the client and leave the team able to keep going.
- An engagement can come first. The subscription keeps the company learning afterward.

## Signature moves and conflict resolution

The Brand Book names “the both/and,” “the practitioner's receipt,” and “the reframe.”
Treat them as reasoning moves:

- Preserve a real tension, then tell the reader what follows from it.
- Ground authority in what Every tried and what happened.
- Replace a stale frame with one that improves the decision.

Do not turn these into repeated sentence templates. Current Every editorial guidance
flags literal correlatives, “not X but Y” reversals, cute `signal, not noise` pairs, and
“receipts” as common AI tells. State the positive claim directly. The Brand Book's
working vocabulary also lists `utilize`; current usage should prefer `use`.

## Words

### Reach for when they are exact

Practice, philosophy, scaffold, learn, play, frontier, pattern, craft, signal, build,
ship, surface, thesis, experiment, edge, attunement, discernment.

### Avoid

Leverage, ecosystem, synergy, disrupt, unlock, empower, innovation, revolutionize,
cutting-edge, deep dive, unpack, space, stakeholder, bandwidth, receipts, delve, robust,
landscape, and AI-ubiquitous filler.

## Cadence

- Use short sentences for weight and longer sentences for thought.
- Let one-sentence paragraphs land substantive points.
- Vary sentence openings and lengths.
- Break repeated three-beat rhythms.
- Rewrite language that sounds like a press release, analyst report, or LinkedIn post.
