## Durable lesson

The repeatable pattern is a sharp opening attached to practical instruction or a
concrete story about work Every did. Product promotion benefits when the release teaches
something useful. Personal stories benefit when they still strengthen Every's authority
in agents, writing with AI, compound engineering, or AI-native work.

## Limits

This review does not isolate the effect of topic, opening, format, media, timing, account
growth, distribution, or launch news. It does not prove that the same pattern transfers
to another account. Use the rule as an Every-specific creative prior and evaluate each
post against its assigned job.
