# Compound Marketing Case: Thesis GTM

## Why this case exists

The Thesis: 2027 planning process is the clearest observed GTM baseline. It shows how a
real launch improved after strategy, source authority, protected language, and open
questions were made explicit.

## What happened

The first workflow scored poorly in retrospective review because it:

- Allowed logistics and metrics to crowd out the strategy.
- Used unsupported dates and people-to-topic connections.
- Replaced user-selected campaign language.
- Turned a social brief into an operating manual.
- Treated open pricing and programming questions as decided.

The accepted work restored the After Automation story, the primary application action,
and the Provoke and Promote campaign jobs. It separated the master GTM plan from the
social brief, preserved open questions, and added source and scoped-edit safeguards.

## What it established

- Strategy and audience behavior must precede the calendar.
- A campaign needs a mechanism and causal launch backbone.
- Current facts, protected language, and open questions require different handling.
- Scoped revisions should preserve untouched sections exactly.

These rules now live in `marketing/gtm` and its regression cases.

## Evidence limits

The baseline was reconstructed after the fact. Review time was not measured
prospectively, and the recorded score is partly human judgment rather than a reproducible
end-to-end comparison. This is a real operational case, but not yet compounding proof.

## Next comparable run

The next substantial GTM project should open a prospective run record before drafting,
inherit the Thesis-derived rules, and measure review minutes, first-pass acceptance,
critical defects, and accepted inherited decisions.
