# Compound Strategy

Decide what to do, for whom, and why. Produce the smallest approved strategy that can
direct the requested work, preserving the full argument when a separate executive summary
is needed. Do not turn a settled brief into another strategy exercise.

## Start at the unresolved decision

Read `../../foundation/marketing-os/SKILL.md` as foundation, not as a request to restart
routing. For strategic decisions, read its method and collaboration references; an exact
scoped correction inherits the approved strategy. Use `../../foundation/marketing-os/references/task-context.md`
for the brief and any clean handoff. Establish which sources own facts,
strategic choices, exact wording, and approval. Retrieve available evidence before asking
the human for a decision the sources cannot settle.

Choose only the needed specialist:

| Need | Existing specialist |
|---|---|
| Research or audience uncertainty | `marketing-science/research`, `marketing-science/archetyping` |
| New or rebuilt brand strategy | `strategy/compound-brand`, strategic-foundation phase |
| Revise an established position | `positioning/{relevant brand}` |
| Message hierarchy and audience propositions | `strategy/messaging-architecture` |
| Choose or scope a recurring program | `strategy/program-brief` |
| Concise executive decision | `strategy/one-pager` |

Paths in this table are repository-relative; read the selected `SKILL.md`. Missing or
scaffolded brand modules are not evidence. Use current approved sources and name gaps.

Lock the diagnosis, audience, desired change, guiding choice, value or offer, proof,
strategic refusal, and success criterion at the level needed for the assignment.
Separate facts, hypotheses, approved choices, and unresolved questions. Preserve exact
human-approved language; a summary does not replace the full strategy.

## Boundaries and handoff

Strategy owns enduring choices and program investment. Compound GTM owns their market
execution: channel roles, distribution, sequence, owners, and dates. A campaign concept
may be developed with Copywriting and Art Direction, but it cannot silently change the
approved audience, offer, or strategic premise.

A full brand-build request may continue through Compound Brand's approved phases into
verbal identity and Art Direction. A positioning-only request stops at positioning;
it does not force a brand book, launch, or identity project.

For multi-discipline work, pass the existing decision-record path and version to the next
workflow. Do not create a competing record. For a small standalone assignment, the
approved brief and source links are enough.
