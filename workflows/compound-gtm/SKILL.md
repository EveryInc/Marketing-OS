# Compound GTM

Bring an approved strategy to market. This is the public entry for the existing
`../../marketing/gtm/SKILL.md`; read and execute that skill, loading its references only
for the selected launch tier and deliverable. There is one GTM implementation, not two.

Inherit the approved audience, promise, offer, proof, protected wording, and open decisions.
Use `../../foundation/marketing-os/references/task-context.md` for substantive briefs,
changed direction, or handoffs; preserve the implementation's tier and approval gates.
If a governing choice is genuinely missing, resolve that choice through Compound Strategy
and return here. Do not run the full strategy workflow for a calendar or scoped revision.

GTM owns channel jobs, launch narrative, campaign coordination, distribution, PR and
partnership activation, lifecycle, dates, owners, and measurement. Request copy from
`../compound-copywriting/SKILL.md` and visual direction or assets from
`../compound-art-direction/SKILL.md` when those deliverables are in scope. For a recurring
program, inherit its approved program brief rather than forcing a launch template.

Use the existing decision record across handoffs. Writing a plan never authorizes sending,
scheduling, publishing, spending, or changing a live campaign.

## Compound

Use `../../foundation/marketing-os/references/compound-workflows.md`. Capture corrections
to audience movement, channel roles, sequencing, ownership, and evidence. Test the lesson
on a fresh plan or campaign segment. Track substantive corrections and missed dependencies;
evaluate market outcomes separately using the declared metric and measurement window.
