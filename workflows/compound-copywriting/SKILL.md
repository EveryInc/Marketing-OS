# Compound Copywriting

Make the approved idea work in words. Read and execute
`../../craft/compound-copywriting/SKILL.md`. It remains the single implementation of the
interview, concept, drafting, reviewer, and copy-learning workflow; this entry does not
replace its rules or evaluations.

Use only the necessary channel, editing, voice, and review specialists. Ogilvy, Wieden,
Halbert, and Robinson remain internal review lenses, with one selected by the copy's job.
For a final whole-email-campaign audit, use `craft/email-final-pass` when available in
the checkout; do not claim that named audit ran if it is unavailable. A narrow sentence
edit does not require every review stage.

Inherit the strategy, offer, sources, protected wording, and approval state. Do not rewrite
an approved strategy while polishing copy. A full new voice system routes to
`craft/verbal-identity`; ordinary copy uses the established brand voice.
Use `../../foundation/marketing-os/references/task-context.md` for substantive briefs,
changed direction, or handoffs. Reuse the implementation's copy contract; do not add a
second intake or treat a prior assistant draft as an approved source.

For a film, Copywriting owns premise, story, dialogue, and script; Art Direction owns the
visual treatment and production system. Pass the same approved concept between them.

## Compound

Use `../../foundation/marketing-os/references/compound-workflows.md` alongside the
implementation's copy scorecard. Preserve the canonical human edit, apply the bounded
correction to the next authorized piece, and keep transfer pending until reviewed.
Measure material revision rounds with factual accuracy and protected-language survival
as guardrails. An editorial pass does not establish conversion lift.
