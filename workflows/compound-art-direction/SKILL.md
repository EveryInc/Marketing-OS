# Compound Art Direction

Turn approved strategy into a coherent visual system and finished applications. Read
`../../craft/art-direction/SKILL.md` and use its relevant references. It remains the
authoritative visual-method and production implementation.

## Enter at the right stage

- **Design fundamentals or beginner assistance:** use the underlying skill's design
  foundations route. Explain the relevant principle through the requested surface;
  do not require a new identity process for a lesson or a layout repair.
- **New identity or territory:** use the full reference, competitive-audit, territory,
  human-selection, and representative-surface process.
- **Existing approved system:** inherit its concept, references, components, fonts,
  assets, and constraints. Skip territory discovery and new reference collection unless
  a consequential visual choice has been reopened.
- **Critique or repair:** inspect the actual source and final output, identify the
  smallest supported correction, and preserve approved content and unaffected design.

These entry modes govern which implementation stages apply. The reference-collection
gate in the underlying skill applies to new visual direction; an approved system already
supplies that evidence. Missing approved references or a changed concept reopens the gate.

Use the relevant Figma, image-generation, slide, or asset-production tool only when the
assignment needs it. The workflow name does not make those tools available. If a tool,
font, or asset is missing, report the specific limit; do not substitute silently or claim
an editable deliverable from a static preview.

Copywriting owns script and verbal expression. Art Direction owns typography, layout,
imagery, visual motion, reusable components, and rendered execution. Neither changes the
approved concept, wording, or offer without surfacing the decision.

## Practical design foundation

For layout, typography, color, or composition decisions, read
`../../craft/art-direction/references/design-foundations.md`. Use its defaults only
where the approved system leaves a gap. Before delivering a designed surface, use
`../../craft/art-direction/references/design-review.md` and inspect the actual render.
Explain unfamiliar terms at first use. Report observed defects separately from creative
recommendations and unverified checks. These references add execution support; they do
not replace strategy, reference selection, human approval, or distinctive expression.

## Compound

Use `../../foundation/marketing-os/references/compound-workflows.md`. Preserve the approved
visual and name the specific correction: crop, type hierarchy, composition, identity,
contrast, motion, or export. Apply the bounded rule to a fresh authorized asset and inspect
it at its destination size. Check editable structure and rendered output separately.
Track material correction rounds and production defects. A valid file or component tree
alone does not prove visual quality or successful transfer.
