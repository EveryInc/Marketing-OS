# AI-native control points and interface grammar

Use this reference when product, enterprise, education, or marketing strategy must decide
where Every can build durable value around AI, or when a product surface presents many
agent-generated items for people to scan, compare, approve, or route.

## The strategic lens

Model capability is expected to diffuse across providers and products. Do not treat model
access, a temporary benchmark lead, or a thin interface as a durable advantage. Ask where
repeated work must pass before intelligence becomes useful to a person or organization.

A candidate control point may include:

- A trusted customer relationship or distribution channel.
- Persistent working context and completed artifacts.
- Permissions, approvals, and exception handling.
- Workflow history that improves future execution.
- Shared vocabulary, formats, and operating protocols.
- Proprietary evals or feedback tied to consequential work.
- Deep integrations or contracts that make the system part of ordinary operations.

Treat these as hypotheses. A control point becomes credible only when evidence shows that
activity repeatedly passes through it, customers depend on it, and increased AI use makes
the position stronger rather than easier to replace. Temporary scarcity and high margins
do not prove durability. Political, platform, and model-provider claims must also be
considered.

Prefer strategies that strengthen Every's approved system of Ideas + Software + Education,
connected by community and brought inside companies through enterprise. Do not infer that
Every should train a foundation model or imitate the capital structure of a model lab.

## Human attention is a control point

As machine output becomes abundant, human attention, comprehension, and approval remain
scarce. The interface between generated work and the person responsible for acting on it
can become strategically important. Product and enterprise plans should specify:

- What the person must notice first.
- Which action, status, owner, exception, or evidence must be compared across items.
- Which decisions require human approval and which can proceed automatically.
- How uncertainty, failure, and escalation appear.
- How the person recovers context or inspects the underlying source.

Do not optimize for the amount of text produced. Optimize for the person's ability to
understand the state of work and take the correct next action.

## Grammar for repeated agent output

Apply these rules to task lists, session histories, inbox summaries, approval queues,
status reports, dashboards, and other collections of generated text:

1. **Match normal sentence parsing.** Begin with the subject or action. When the subject
   repeats across every item, omit it and begin with the verb.
2. **Put distinguishing information first.** Assume readers may scan only the opening
   words. Lead with the status, action, exception, or object that separates one item from
   the others.
3. **Keep structure and vocabulary consistent.** Use one sentence pattern across
   comparable items and one term for each concept.
4. **Make variation semantic.** Differences in length, structure, color, or vocabulary
   should correspond to meaningful differences in the underlying state.
5. **Choose verbs that reveal purpose.** A verb should make clear whether the item reports
   progress, requests action, raises an exception, records approval, or describes praise
   or failure.

Preserve source access and meaningful nuance. A template that makes every item look tidy
while hiding uncertainty or exceptions has failed.

These rules do not govern singular creative artifacts. Essays, headlines, campaign lines,
scripts, and personal correspondence may require surprise, rhythm, ambiguity, or variation.
Do not standardize them merely because AI helped produce them.

## Product and enterprise decisions

For products, build these rules into schemas, interface components, and evals instead of
relying on a long prompt to produce consistent prose. The system should own the format.

For enterprise work, help the organization define a shared operating language for agent
status, ownership, evidence, approval, exception handling, and escalation. Generic AI
training is weaker than a tested protocol connected to consequential workflows.

For education and editorial, teach people to design systems of machine output and human
supervision, not only to improve an individual response.

## Measurement

Choose one decision metric before testing a repeated-output interface:

- Correct triage or routing after a short scan.
- Time to identify the status, owner, next action, or exception.
- Approval accuracy or avoidable rework.

Diagnostic measures may include rereads, source opens, mistaken approvals, missed
exceptions, and vocabulary inconsistency. Guard against omitted nuance, hidden uncertainty,
and automation that reduces a person's ability to intervene.

Do not claim the interface has become a durable control point from a usability improvement
alone. Durability requires repeated-use, retention, switching, or contractual evidence.
