# Four workflows, one learning protocol

Marketing OS is the umbrella. Its public workflows are Compound Strategy, Compound GTM,
Compound Copywriting, and Compound Art Direction. Each can run alone. Cross-discipline
work uses one approved brief or decision record, not four independent intake processes.

## Ownership

| Workflow | Owns | Hands off |
|---|---|---|
| Strategy | Audience, diagnosis, positioning, strategic choices, program investment | Approved direction, evidence, boundaries, open decisions |
| GTM | Market execution, channel roles, distribution, lifecycle, calendar, owners | Production briefs and measurement plan |
| Copywriting | Verbal concepts, draft copy, scripts, editing, copy review | Reviewed words and protected language |
| Art Direction | Visual concepts, identity systems, production, rendered verification | Approved visual rules and editable assets |

Concept development can involve more than one discipline. Choose a lead from the requested
deliverable and pass one brief between collaborators. Never duplicate strategy discovery
or reopen approved choices merely because another workflow joined the task.

Research, measurement, brand context, source authority, and the theoretical foundation are
shared capabilities. Load them for the decision at hand; do not create extra public
workflows or require the entire library for a small edit. Existing specialist paths remain
valid. `strategy/compound-marketing` is compatibility and run coordination, not a fifth
peer workflow. Historical record paths and decision IDs stay unchanged.

## Make, review, capture, apply, check

1. Produce the requested work from approved sources and constraints.
2. Review it through the relevant specialist and human approval requirements.
3. When a meaningful human correction or result exists, preserve the before/after
   references and describe the smallest instruction it supports.
4. Apply that instruction to fresh, comparable work within the user's requested scope.
5. Record the observed result, including failures and missing evidence.

Use `learning-loop.md` for capture and promotion, and `knowledge-boundaries.md` for what
may enter durable guidance. Do not invent learning from the model's own critique or add
work solely to manufacture a transfer test. If no fresh task exists, mark transfer pending.

The record may be a brief note for a small assignment. For an end-to-end project or an
explicit benchmark, use `golden-path.md` and the existing Compound Marketing run format.
Reuse any open matching run; do not start a new record for each discipline. Keep private
source text and project evidence outside this reusable repository.

## Match the evidence to the discipline

| Workflow | Useful correction measure | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| Strategy | Substantive rebriefs or human correction time | Approved choices, supporting evidence, explicit uncertainty |
| GTM | Material plan revisions or missed dependencies | Decision fidelity, owners, dates, offer and channel accuracy |
| Copywriting | Material revision rounds | Human quality, factual accuracy, protected wording |
| Art Direction | Material visual revisions or production defects | Human visual approval, identity, editable structure, rendered output |

Choose the relevant measure before comparison; missing baselines remain missing.
Do not add unlike scores into a single Marketing OS grade. Brand, demand, adoption, and
conversion outcomes are evaluated separately with the measurement specialist.

Distinguish **captured**, **applied**, **human-reviewed transfer**, and **measured improvement**.
The existing proof standard still governs improvement claims: comparable prospective runs,
at least 20% less correction or review time with no quality regression, and an independent
teammate rerun before claiming team reuse. Renaming a skill establishes none of these.
