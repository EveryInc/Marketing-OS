# ChatGPT Ads

Platform facts change quickly. Each item carries the date it was checked; verify in Ads
Manager or OpenAI's documentation before relying on it.

## Unit and placement (checked November 2026)

- Ads appear below ChatGPT answers for Free and Go users only; paid plans see no ads
  ([NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/ads-chatgpt-openai-rcna254454))
- Chat card: headline up to 50 characters, description up to 100; OpenAI's recommended
  lengths are shorter. Square image
  ([Strike Social](https://strikesocial.com/blog/the-complete-list-of-chatgpt-ad-specs-2026-update/))
- Turn off creative enhancements to stop automatic rewrites of copy and URLs

## Targeting (checked November 2026)

- No keywords. Advertisers write context hints describing the conversations where the
  offer fits; the system matches against the chat
- Customer lists can include, exclude, or adjust bids; exclusion lists may be small
- Location and platform targeting; no retargeting
- Ads are not shown near sensitive topics such as health and politics

## Landing pages

- OpenAI's crawler reads the landing page to match ads. Login walls and CAPTCHAs block it;
  keep a public preview above any signup wall
  ([AirOps](https://www.airops.com/blog/chatgpt-ads-best-practices))
- Send conversions through both the pixel and the Conversions API with a shared event ID

## Writing for this surface

- The reader is mid-conversation and already using AI. Situation questions and task names
  that match what they just asked fit the moment
- The audience is Free and Go users, so promises must hold on those plans (C6)
- Show the product in the image, not a logo, and nothing that imitates the ChatGPT interface
- A reported $25 US daily minimum (unconfirmed in OpenAI documentation) turns small budgets
  into short bursts; read results per burst
