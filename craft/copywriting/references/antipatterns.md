# Copywriting Antipatterns

Use to diagnose a weak draft after applying `principles.md`. These are editorial failure
patterns, not a ban on every occurrence of a word or device. Preserve deliberate human
choices and apply the active brand's AI-check and style rules.

| Failure | Why it fails | Repair |
|---|---|---|
| A generic announcement leads an unfamiliar product | The reader has no reason to want the news yet. | Lead with the relevant change, tension, or demonstration; establish the product promptly. Keep a direct announcement when the news itself matters. |
| An intriguing hook leads to an unrelated pitch | Attention is earned under a promise the body does not fulfill. | Make the same reader desire govern the opening, proof, and next action. |
| A feature list stands in for an argument | The reader has to infer which capability matters and why. | Choose one proposition and show the capabilities through a relevant task and result. |
| An example is followed by an abstract moral | The copy repeats the inference and drains force from the detail. | Delete the interpretation when the example is clear. Supply missing causal context when it is not. |
| A famous name or large number supplies decorative proof | Prestige is unrelated to the reader's objection. | Use relevant evidence and explain the relationship. Identify internal use, customer testimony, and illustration accurately. |
| Compression removes the cause or qualification | The shorter claim becomes confusing or misleading. | Cut duplicate setup and commentary before the detail, offer condition, or review step that makes the claim true. |
| Superlatives or borrowed ad structures substitute for an idea | The language could advertise anything and may imply unsupported superiority. | Find the product truth or human observation. Study a precedent's operation without inheriting its wording or status. |
| Stock contrasts, solemn fragments, and repeated three-part rhythms manufacture emphasis | Sentence pattern becomes more noticeable than the thought. | State the claim directly, vary the rhythm, and retain a device only when it serves the idea. |
| A fabricated personal story or stock question seeks engagement | The voice and evidence become untrustworthy. | Use an observed situation, useful artifact, or defensible opinion. Ask a question only when its answers matter. |
| Urgency, scarcity, or risk reversal is added to fill a formula | The next action depends on an unsupported condition. | Use the actual event, deadline, offer, and terms; remove an invented reason to act. |
| Platform folklore governs the draft | A character count or link trick crowds out the communication job. | Verify mechanics and test editorial choices against the account's audience and outcomes. |
| Opens or likes are presented as sales evidence | The metric does not establish the claimed behavior or causal effect. | Define the appropriate outcome and denominator; use engagement as a diagnostic where relevant. |

Read the resulting copy as a new reader: what is offered, why would I want it, what earns
belief, and what happens next? Then judge whether the expression has character. A clean
draft can still be dull. Use `formats.md` or `creative-canon.md` to change the approach
when another pass over the wording is insufficient.
