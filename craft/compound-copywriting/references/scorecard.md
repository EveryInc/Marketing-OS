# Compound Copywriting Scorecard

Measure whether the system reduces revision burden while preserving human authorship and
copy quality. Store run records with the project or task, not in reusable doctrine.

## Primary metric

**Material revision rounds per approved artifact.** Count one round when human review
requires a change to the proposition, audience, claims, evidence, protected language,
structure, voice, or channel job. Do not count typo, punctuation, or formatting cleanup.

Compare the median across comparable artifacts. Mark different channels, audiences, and
levels of strategic ambiguity separately when they materially change difficulty.

## Diagnostic metrics

- **Time to approval:** Elapsed time from the first agent draft to human approval. Mark
  long inactive gaps rather than pretending they are production time.
- **Protected-language survival:** Protected items preserved divided by protected items
  supplied. Target 100%.
- **First useful draft:** The first draft contains a load-bearing structure or line that
  survives approval and does not require a wholesale strategic rewrite.
- **Human quality score:** Final approver rates the artifact from 1 to 5 after the copy is
  usable, not after seeing the process explanation.
- **Transfer result:** Pass, fail, or pending on a fresh comparable artifact.

## Hard failures

Record each occurrence. One hard failure prevents a clean pass:

- Invented or laundered fact, claim, quote, price, date, approval, or audience insight.
- Protected or canonical language changed without authorization.
- An open decision converted into approved copy.
- A project-specific edit promoted as durable doctrine without clearing the gate.
- Raw private copy or sensitive source material stored in a repository learning record.
- Improvement claimed without a comparable baseline or fresh transfer test.
- Process commentary delivered in place of the requested copy.

## Decision rules

- **Continue:** Quality is at least 4/5, no hard failure occurred, and the transfer check
  passed or is honestly pending.
- **Revise the system:** The same material failure appears twice, protected-language
  survival falls below 100%, or median revision rounds fail to improve after enough
  comparable work exists.
- **Promote a rule:** The Marketing OS promotion gate clears and the fresh transfer test
  passes.
- **Make no claim:** There is no comparable baseline, quality score, or transfer evidence.

For the first real campaign, establish the baseline. Do not grade improvement against an
imagined pre-system workflow.
