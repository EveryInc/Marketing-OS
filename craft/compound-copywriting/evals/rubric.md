# Compound Copywriting Eval Rubric

Score each completed workflow out of 100.

This rubric grades an executed workflow. A valid case manifest or retrospective case set proves
structural coverage only; it does not prove that an agent followed the rules, that copy improved,
or that the system compounded.

## Scored dimensions

### Human authorship and copy quality: 25

- The human owns the point of view, consequential lines, and final approval.
- An incomplete brief is interviewed before substantial drafting; a complete brief moves
  directly into the work without process ceremony.
- The primary persuasion job is classified before the channel, and one matching reviewer
  is used by default.
- Consequential work with no approved creative approach compares genuinely different
  concepts before a large asset set is drafted.
- The strategic job of each unit is established before prose; redundant slides,
  sections, or messages are cut rather than polished.
- Generated material is selective, specific, grammatical, and channel-fit.
- The opening expresses a relevant desire, tension, benefit, or meaningful piece of news;
  it is not merely accurate announcement language when the brief requires persuasion.
- The body pays off the opening with relevant proof and a credible next step.
- Distinctive observation, expression, or product detail survives compression. Examples
  are not followed by commentary that only restates their meaning.
- The requested copy arrives before process commentary.

### Source and canonical fidelity: 20

- Authority, scope, and protected language are explicit.
- Facts, claims, open decisions, and canonical copy remain intact.
- The human-edited artifact becomes canonical for the project.

### Delta interpretation: 20

- Meaningful edits are classified by operation rather than surface imitation.
- Project-specific taste stays local.
- The carry-forward instruction names its trigger, scope, decision, and boundary.
- Human-edited repeated-unit structure and native emphasis survive in related units.
- Distinct stages such as attention, trial, and adoption remain distinct when the human
  has separated them.

### Transfer and learning integrity: 20

- A fresh comparable artifact tests the learning.
- Project-local application remains separate from durable promotion.
- Durable changes clear the Marketing OS learning and knowledge-boundary gates.

### Measurement honesty: 15

- Material revision rounds use the defined counting rule.
- Baselines and unavailable fields are represented honestly.
- Campaign precedent and platform advice are not presented as causal conversion laws;
  email and social outcomes match the actual objective and tracking limitations.
- Protected-language survival, hard failures, quality, and transfer are recorded.

## Hard failures

Any hard failure prevents a pass:

- Changes protected or canonical language without authorization.
- Invents or launders a fact, claim, quote, price, date, approval, or audience insight.
- Promotes a single project edit without clearing the learning gate.
- Claims learning without a fresh transfer test.
- Stores raw private copy or sensitive project material as reusable doctrine.
- Claims improvement without a comparable baseline.
- Returns process ceremony instead of the requested copy.
- Invents missing brief decisions or forces a complete brief through an unnecessary interview.
- Routes review by container alone, uses more than one named reviewer on an artifact without
  an explicit panel or documented consequential conflict, or imitates a named copywriter's
  prose.
- Starts Judge, Learn, Test, or Compound before a meaningful human edit, rejection,
  selection, restoration, or other observable correction exists.
- Invokes a reviewer without passing the brief, artifact, protected language, proof sources,
  desired reader movement, and selected concept when applicable.
- Calls an untested draft high-converting, or transfers another campaign's uplift to it.
- Fills a public proof slot with an unaudited workflow or repeats an example where a fresh
  approved example was explicitly required.
- Ignores the human-edited schema for sibling cards, including requested count or native
  formatting.

## Passing bar

Require at least 85 points and zero hard failures.

For email/social changes, run the applicable cases in `persuasion-cases.json` as well as
the original `cases.json`. Grade observable outputs, not mention of the rules. Structural
validation is not a behavioral pass, and an editorial pass is not a market-performance claim.
