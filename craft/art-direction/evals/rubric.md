# Art Direction Eval Rubric

Score each completed artifact or workflow out of 100.

## Scored dimensions

### Strategic traceability: 25

- The visual direction follows an approved argument, audience, and intended behavior.
- Formal choices can be traced to the governing idea.
- Applications prove the system instead of becoming isolated decoration.

### Reference and semiotic rigor: 20

- Human references and anti-references are specific and analyzed.
- Category codes are separated from distinctive opportunities.
- Territories combine reference domains without copying them literally.

### System quality: 20

- Typography, color, composition, imagery, motion, material, and accessibility behave as
  one system.
- The system can produce varied surfaces without losing recognition.
- Edge cases and failure modes are defined.

### Human authorship and taste: 15

- The human controls reference selection, territory choice, and final approval.
- Ambiguous taste decisions are surfaced rather than averaged away.
- Corrections sharpen future rules without overgeneralizing an exception.

### Production fitness: 20

- Canonical components, assets, fields, fonts, variables, and owners are explicit.
- Labels are semantic, stable, and unambiguous.
- Tool boundaries and handoffs preserve fidelity.
- Structural and rendered QA both pass.

## Hard failures

Any hard failure prevents a pass:

- Generates a final identity from generic aesthetic labels without the human reference gate.
- Develops assets before a territory is approved.
- Silently substitutes a font, asset, identity, or unresolved decision.
- Treats raw AI-generated imagery as approved final art.
- Confuses a good-looking output with verified identity and structure.
- Declares the workflow complete without visual proof.
- Replaces a cheap, judgment-heavy human correction with a brittle automation detour.

## Passing bar

Require at least 80 points and zero hard failures.
