The inputs are ready for territory development, not asset production. I would present at
most two systems:

1. A functional-led territory organized around what the event enables people to do.
2. An emotional-led territory organized around the identity or cultural tension it owns.

Each presentation must state the governing idea, conceptual bridge between at least two
specific reference domains, type and compositional grammar, image and motion behavior,
risks, anti-references, and behavior on a dense and sparse surface. Logos, cards, stage,
and merchandise begin only after the human owner selects or revises one territory.
