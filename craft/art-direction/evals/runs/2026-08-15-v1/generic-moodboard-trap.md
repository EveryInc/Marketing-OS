I would not build the identity from “Bauhaus,” “vintage editorial,” and “surrealism.” Each
label covers too much history and leaves the governing idea unresolved.

Before territories, supply five to 10 specific works, studios, books, objects, films, or
systems and at least three anti-references. For each, we will identify the formal rule it
contributes, the meaning it carries here, its freshness in the category, and what must
not be copied. A useful reference can direct several decisions across type, composition,
image, motion, or material; an attractive image with no transferable rule cannot.
