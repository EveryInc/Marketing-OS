The field's black grounds, neon gradients, chrome, orbs, and abstract 3D establish
“technology event” quickly, but together they have become category wallpaper.

- **Retain:** High contrast and motion cues when they clarify live technology.
- **Reject:** Chrome, glowing orbs, and anonymous 3D as default atmosphere.
- **Invert:** Use light or material grounds and flat, authored imagery where competitors
  use depth without meaning.
- **Replace:** Build recognition from a specific conference behavior, participant role,
  or intellectual mechanism.

The white space is a conference identity that shows how ideas enter, assemble, and alter
a public system. Territories should follow only after the event strategy and specific
reference set are approved.
